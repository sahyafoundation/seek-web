Society for Environmental Education in Kerala

Edat, Payyanur, Kannur

The Society for Environmental Education in Kerala, widely known as SEEK, is one of Kerala's pioneering non-governmental organizations dedicated to grassroots environmental education, nature conservation, and ecological research.

Based in Edat, Payyanur, SEEK has spent decades working alongside local communities and youth to foster deep-rooted environmental literacy since 1981.
